How to use in real-life projects

  1. Deploy the app (static hosting or small VPS) and configure a proper backend (optional) to store bookings centrally instead of localStorage.
  2. Integrate OAuth (Google/OTP) for smoother sign-in, and replace client-side hashing with secure server-side authentication for production.
  3. Connect to third-party APIs (flights, hotels, maps) to provide live prices and availability instead of sample data.
  4. Use an email/SMS service to send booking confirmations and PNRs to users.
  5. Add role-based access and admin dashboards for reporting, and move exports to server-side generation for larger datasets.
  6. Ensure privacy and compliance (secure storage of user data, TLS, backups).

Features of AI Travel Planner app

  • Interactive slide-based destination presentation with maps and quick links.
  • Local demo search for hotels, flights, buses and cabs with quick booking buttons.
  • User authentication (signup/login) with admin role and session preservation.
  • Booking creation, local storage, PDF ticket generation with QR code.
  • Admin panel: view bookings, export CSV, delete entries, and download tickets.
  • Itinerary generator and AI suggestions for each destination (sample rules/phrases).
  • Export and reporting capabilities (CSV) for offline analysis.
  • Extensible front-end to integrate real APIs and backend services for production.
